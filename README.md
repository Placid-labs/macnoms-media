# Macnoms Media — Studio Website

High-converting, editorial one-page studio website for **Macnoms Media** — a premium growth consultancy helping ambitious brands turn attention into community, customers, and compounding growth.

---

## Brand Positioning & Design System
- **Core Motto:** *"We help ambitious brands turn attention into community, customers and growth."*
- **Disciplines:** Growth &bull; Community &bull; Digital Strategy
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Editorial luxury agency with a warm cream background (`#FAF7F2`), deep burgundy typography (`#3D0C14`), and champagne gold accents (`#C5A880`).
- **Typography:** *Playfair Display* (Editorial Serif) paired with *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Clean Modern Grotesque).

---

## Sections Included
1. **Header:** Sticky navigation with brand wordmark, positioning tag, and quick-action buttons.
2. **01 — Hero:** Editorial headline (*"Your brand deserves more than just attention."*), lead description, action buttons, and magazine-cover style visual collage.
3. **02 — The Problem:** 5-stage interactive conversion funnel (`ATTENTION → INTEREST → COMMUNITY → ACTION → GROWTH`).
4. **03 — What We Do:** 5 core service capabilities (Growth Strategy, Social & Content, Community, Influencer Marketing, Campaigns & Projects) with key deliverables.
5. **04 — Why Macnoms:** Strategic breakdown of the intersection between **People**, **Product**, and **Growth**.
6. **05 — Selected Case Studies:**
   - **WOOMEOUT:** 5K+ sign-ups generated for social/dating platform.
   - **WARRI VENDORS:** 11K+ community growth & experiential market activations.
   - **CLIENT CAMPAIGN:** 1M+ organic views via viral cultural distribution.
   - **INFLUENCER CAMPAIGNS:** 6-Stage creator execution framework.
   - Interactive case study detail modal.
7. **06 — How We Work:** 4-step framework (*Discover &bull; Strategise &bull; Execute &bull; Optimise*).
8. **07 — Services & Packages:**
   - Clarity Audit: ₦50K
   - Growth Sprint: ₦100K
   - Growth & Community: ₦175K/month
   - Growth Partner: From ₦300K/month
   - Quick summary badge (*Projects from ₦50K &bull; Retainers from ₦175K/month*).
9. **08 — Who We Work With:** Infinite marquee ticker (*STARTUPS &bull; FOUNDERS &bull; LIFESTYLE &bull; BEAUTY &bull; FASHION &bull; EVENTS &bull; DIGITAL PRODUCTS &bull; CREATORS*).
10. **11 — High-Converting Closing CTA:** Deep luxury burgundy canvas with huge cream typography, primary action button, and direct contacts (`hello@macnomsmedia.com`, Instagram, LinkedIn).
11. **12 — Footer:** Clean copyright, studio attribution, and back-to-top navigation.
12. **Project Intake Modal:** Interactive client brief submission with service selection and celebratory confetti.
